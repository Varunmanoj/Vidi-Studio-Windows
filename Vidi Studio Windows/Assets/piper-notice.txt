Piper text-to-speech
====================

Vidi Studio includes the Piper speech engine (piper.exe and its runtime files)
from the rhasspy/piper project, release 2023.11.14-2.

Piper is Copyright (c) 2022 Rhasspy and contributors and is licensed under the
MIT License. The full source is available at:

https://github.com/rhasspy/piper
