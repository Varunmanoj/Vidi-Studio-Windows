## 2026-10-26 - Windowed dialogs use the app surface with no floating card or border (1.0.137 / 1.0.137.0)

Every Vidi Studio dialog that opens in its own window now shares the app's normal window chrome. This covers the delete-generated dialog, confirm/error prompts, rename, export information, project-file issues, narration voice/failure reports, the segment editor, New Project, Export, both Piper voice dialogs, and the Gemini key-deletion confirmation because they all call `WindowedDialog.ShowAsync`.
